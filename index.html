    Settings

    Light mode Brighter screen for daytime. Off = dark mode.
    Auto screen lock In shot mode, lock after a short idle so pocket taps do nothing. Swipe to unlock. Default on.
    Auto-mark shots GPS settle only — no motion required. Stand still after walking to the ball; dots count down to mark.
    Audible mark beeps N beeps = shot number. Best with screen on — locked-phone audio is not reliable in browsers.
    Hands-off preference Default new rounds to Hands-off: start near a known tee, flexible hole count, audio cues.
    Achievements ★ Floating stars for drives, birdies, and personal bests. Tap the star to celebrate.
    Beginner tips Occasional one-time hints (map zones, green tap). Default on. Tap to dismiss.
    Green tap putts Near the pin (on-green radius), hybrid pauses — short impulse taps ×3 mark a putt (not shakes).
    Voice chimes (stub) Reserved for spoken cues later. Chimes and beeps work today.
    Auto-mark sensitivity

    Motion stop peak + hold time arm the shot. A swing marks immediately at that spot; walking away only plays a confirm chime. On-green radius is exact (no GPS padding) so chips ~5 m from the pin stay outside tap zone. Map shows tee/green circles to scale.

    Backup & restore

    Courses, rounds, settings, and map photos live in browser storage. Clearing site data or reinstalling the app removes them — download a backup file first and keep it in Downloads or cloud storage.

    About

    Shot Tracker started as a way to keep score and distances on the course without accounts, ads, or sending your rounds to a server. GPS marks and maps stay on your phone.

    The idea is simple tracking you can trust in the pocket: tee off, mark, hole out, review later. Share the live link; each device keeps its own data.

    Shot Tracker is free to use and will stay free — no paywall, no subscription.

    If Shot Tracker helped your round and you want to shout me a coffee, thank you for backing the project.

    Calibration mode

    Record GPS + motion for tuning auto-mark. Hands-free: start, walk your protocol, stop & export JSON to share for analysis.

    Container A/B/C/D protocol (~10 min)

    Loop 2m→6m→2m→6m at each corner:

    • A — stop 8–10s, no swing (×3 slow)
    • B — stop, 1 pretend stroke, stand 5s (×3 slow)
    • C — 2–3 practice swings, no hit (×2)
    • D — short shuffle + soft taps (×2)
    • A+B — repeat at vigorous walk (×3)

    Same pocket every time. Keep the screen awake (app will try to hold it). Optional: tap recording screen to drop a marker.

    Show score bars in play GPS / motion / combined % on the play screen (debug).

        Help

        Fairway auto-mark — Stand still until armed, then your swing marks at that GPS spot. Walking away only plays a confirm chime (it does not wait to mark).

        Min shot move — Swings too close to the last mark are ignored (practice swings).

        On the green — Near the pin, hybrid pauses. Three short phone taps mark a putt (not shakes). Calibrate taps in Settings. Once locked, the zone stays sticky past a GPS bump or a short roll off the green.

        Next tee — After hole-out, walk to the next tee (solid white circle), stop, then auto-tee. Don’t expect tee-off on the previous green.

        Map circles — Tee: solid = near-tee; dashed = leave buffer. Green: solid = enter / lock green-tap; dashed = sticky leave (~150% of enter).

        Club suggest — If you haven’t picked a club, the app suggests your usual club for that hole/shot when known, otherwise a club for the distance to the pin. Highlighted = autofill — tap to change.

        Hole names — Optional: long-press a hole in the hole picker to set a custom name (default is Hole N).

        Pocket lock — Idle lock in shot mode; swipe to unlock.
